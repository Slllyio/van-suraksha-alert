
=== Slide 1 ===
Pilot Project: Rudraprayag Forest Division, 2020

=== Slide 2 ===
Phase 1: Development of Google Maps based application and dashboard with GIS/Mapping of the location of all assets critical to wildfire management including permanent fire lines, crew stations, watch towers, fire service stations, van panchayats, forest nurseries, plantations, water tanker stations etc. on an interactive Google map along with the vegetation type for a Forest Division (Mapping of assets critical for decision making and fire fighting).

=== Slide 3 ===
Phase 2: Linking the mobile application and dashboard with FSI fire alerts and providing fire alerts to all stakeholders through mobile application (instead of relying solely on FSI sms). (sending real-time fire alerts on app to reduce the response time of all stakeholders in managing fires.)

=== Slide 4 ===
Phase 3:  Calibration of the Fire Weather Index for local conditions followed by its integration in the mobile application for communicating the fire danger alerts to all stakeholders including communities that are actively involved in fire management. (Communicating Fire Danger Rating to Public and other stakeholders)
[image]

=== Slide 5 ===
Functionality of the components of this system
Dashboard
Mapping of all forest types, crown density, sensitive areas, assets and infrastructures including crew stations.
Linking of FSI SMS fire alert system with the dashboard and app for automatic fire location updating on the google map.
Real time tracking of fire crew vehicles.
Receiving and verifying new fire alert as received from the public.
Sending warning messages to app users.
Monitoring the status of all fires in the division like unattended fires, being contained fires and extinguished fires.
Taking feedback from field staff through app and updating fire status.
Identifying the first respondent in case of new fire detection.
In case of multiple fires, prioritizing the allocation of resources.
Taking tactical decisions about active fires.
Maintaining database of fires detected and contained.
Android based app
Showing the location of active fires to stakeholders like Govt. Departments , field staff and volunteers, van panchayats etc.
Communicating warning messages and fire weather index to field staff & public.
Providing a platform for fire reporting by public through geo tagged images.
Updating the action taken by field staff on real time basis including the response time in reaching the fire site and extinguishing the fire.
Being used to provide feedback to the MCR dashboard about the status of fires including area burnt.
