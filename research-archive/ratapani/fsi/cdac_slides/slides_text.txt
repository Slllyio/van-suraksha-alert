===== SLIDE 6 =====
                       RATIONALE – Forest Fire Spread Forecasting

• India has a system to disseminate satellite-based fire alerts to the forest department and
  providing the weekly Forest Fire Danger Ratings

• What India doesn’t have is the national level operational forest fire spread forecasting system,
  which can provide forest fire spread forecast for next 24/48 hours

• Despite India having significant High-Performance Computing (HPC) capabilities under National
  Super Computing Mission (NSM) it has not been utilized till date for generating forest fire spread
  forecasts at National level

• The HPC utilization is required for decision grade forest fire spread forecasts using coupled
  atmosphere-forest fire spread forecast models

• The Sikkim Forest department has used the HPC based forest fire spread forecast for the first
  time in the country for live fire and carried out ground operations based on that. The department
  found the system useful to safeguard the forests from further burning

                                                                                Fire propagation

The level set method has been used to estimate fire propagation after ignition.

The evolution of the level set function is governed by the partial differential
equation1,2.

 ∂ψ ∂t + S∥∇ψ∥ = 0

where ψ is the level set function, s is the fire spread rate, and t is time.

===== SLIDE 23 =====
MoU between FSI and C-DAC (in process)
        The specific objectives are to:
          a) Develop scientifically robust and scalable methodologies for modelling and
          forecasting forest fire occurrence, spread, intensity, and risk across diverse forest
          ecosystems of India.
          b) Integrate multi-source datasets, including satellite observations, weather and
          climate data, topographical information, vegetation characteristics, and historical
          fire records, for enhanced prediction and decision support.
          c) Design, test, validate, and refine AI/ML-based models and simulation
          frameworks for near real-time forest fire forecasting and early warning applications.
          d) Facilitate research collaboration, knowledge exchange, and joint innovation in
          the domains of forest fire science, geospatial analytics, artificial intelligence, and
          high-performance computing.
          e) Strengthen institutional capacities through training, workshops, technical
          consultations, and skill development programmes for scientists, researchers, and
          technical personnel.
          f) Promote technology transfer and adoption of developed methodologies, tools,
          and systems for operational use by relevant stakeholders.
          g) Establish mechanisms for data sharing, scientific cooperation, and
          dissemination of research outcomes in accordance with applicable laws, policies,
          and mutually agreed terms.
          h) Contribute towards strengthening national capabilities for forest fire prevention,
          preparedness, mitigation, and response through evidence-based decision support
          systems.

                                CURRENT ACTIVITIES



• Time optimization for forest fire spread forecasting: Being carried out by HPC-Tech team
  – As of now the simulation time has been optimized to 17 minutes using 16 nodes for 24
  hours simulation (further experiments ongoing)

• Multi-ignition-point ingestion in WRF SFIRE. As of now 200 points have been ingested for
  simulation (experimental). The results are being analysed. WRF SFIRE codes are being
  changed to make the WRF SFIRE compatible for Indian needs

• The MoU signing between C-DAC and Forest Survey of India (FSI) for national level
  implementation of ‘Forest Fire Spread Forecasting’ is underway
