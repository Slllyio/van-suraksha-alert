## Known weaknesses

**Cold-start blind spot.** The model excels at fire contagion (Hawkes-driven, 25.5% R@5% in active regions) but is near-random on new outbreaks in quiet regions. 77% of fires actually start in low-Hawkes cells.

**Mitigation:** the operational pipeline applies a **CLIMATOLOGY FLOOR** decoration layer — any cell whose 5-year week-of-year fire rate exceeds the 80th percentile gets auto-promoted to WATCH. See [[02-Operations/Daily Alert Pipeline]] for details.
